FFmpeg 9.0.2 (ffmpeg.exe) - https://ffmpeg.org
  Licensed under the GNU LGPL v2.1 or later. Built without GPL or nonfree
  components, statically linked, with libdav1d (BSD 2-clause).
  FFmpeg source: https://ffmpeg.org/download.html
  Build configuration:
  --enable-static --disable-shared --disable-everything --enable-small
  --disable-gpl --disable-nonfree --enable-libdav1d (plus a minimal set of
  decoders, demuxers, filters and the GIF/BMP encoders).
